Music is a passion of mine

From as early as I can remember, I've been listening to music. When I was a child, I learned to appreciate the humor in the world around me largely through Weird "Al" Yancovich I was able to explore many genres, music styles, artists, and humorus connections to things around me through his discography. As I grew, I learned that I loved the range of emotions that could be captured by a guitar and I discovered Jack White through The White Stripes. Their iconic edgy, offbeat, emotional, modern, blues-rock fusion defines my music taste to this day. In middle school, I found Eminem, whose vocals, lyrical ingenuity, and struggle let me see the scope of the world around me and better relate to those around me. Later in highschool, I found NOFX while I was looking for something faster than the blues-rock I had been listening to. I remember I was shown one of their songs in highschool when my friend Jack wore Birkenstocks to class, and my teacher said that reminded him of a band he used to listen to that had a song called "Jeff Wears Birkenstocks". I was captivated by the drums, the guitar, the vocals, just everything about that song resonated with me at the time
