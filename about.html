Music is a passion of mine

From an early age, music has played a pivotal role in my life. My appreciation for the humor and nuance in the world was significantly shaped by Weird Al Yankovic. His diverse discography allowed me to explore a variety of genres, styles, and the humorous connections that fill our lives.

As I grew older, I discovered my love for the emotional depth that a guitar can convey, ignited by Jack White and The White Stripes. Their unique blend of edgy, offbeat modern blues-rock continues to influence my musical taste profoundly.

In middle school, Eminem introduced me to a wider perspective of the world through his powerful vocals and lyrical ingenuity, helping me relate to the challenges people face around me.

Later in high school, while in search of something with a quicker tempo than the blues-rock I enjoyed, I stumbled upon NOFX. A vivid memory of mine will forever be being introduced to NOFX when my friend, Jack, wore Birkenstocks to class, reminding the teacher of a song he had to show us. The song was none other than NOFX's "Jeff Wears Birkenstocks". The captivating blend of drums, guitar, and vocals in their music resonated with me instantly.

In college, I found folk-punk. This genre is very raw, emotional, and fast. Many of them sing of the struggles of mental health, poverty, and thinking differently than how you are expected to think.

I have also recently fallen in love with Hyperpop, Horror Rap, and Scene music. These experiences collectively shape my eclectic taste in music, reflecting a journey of discovery and connection.
